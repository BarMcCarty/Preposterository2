  Integers are what are regularly referred to as "counting numbers", that is, the simplest step-by-step numbers you can use
to measure wholes. This would include counting positives, like 1, 2, 3, and so on forever. It would also include the opposite;
negative numbers are also integers, yet it goes backwards. The sum of two integers is easily described as total distance from
zero. If you looked at a number line, and were told to add 2 + 3, you could start from zero and move to 2, and then move 3
positions to the right relative to 2. It also works with negatives, except positive numbers strictly move right and negatives 
will move left. An example of this is 17 plus -8. Start from zero and move right to the 17th positive position. Then, relative
to 17, move left 8 places, which will leave you at 9.
